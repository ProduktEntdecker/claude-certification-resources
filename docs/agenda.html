HostFlorian Steiner, Claude Community Ambassador Munich

Florian Steiner is a Claude Community Ambassador in Munich and helps agencies, consultancies and SMEs work productively with Claude.

Who this is for

For all, especially people at Claude Partner Network organisations who are considering certification. The room is used before and after this session, so we start and end on time.

What you will leave with

  1. 1Know the four Claude certifications, who each one is for, and what it takes to pass.
  2. 2Understand the Claude Partner Network path and who is eligible to take the exams.
  3. 3Leave with a preparation plan and a set of resources to start studying.
