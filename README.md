# Claude Certification Resources

This repository accompanies the session **"Claude Certification Programme for Partners"** by Florian Steiner at the Claude Community House, Barcelona, on 23 September 2026. It collects the public, official facts about Anthropic's Claude certifications and the Claude Partner Network (CPN), plus links to preparation material, so that Claude Community Ambassadors, partners, and consultancies have one place to start from.

> **Disclaimer.** This is an independent, community-maintained resource. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by Anthropic**. Fees, eligibility rules, exam formats, and program details change; always confirm the current facts on the official pages linked in this README (section 7) before you register, study, or advise a client. Everything here reflects the state of the linked pages as retrieved on 14 September 2026.

The session agenda with timeline is in [`docs/agenda.html`](docs/agenda.html) (PDF: [`docs/agenda.pdf`](docs/agenda.pdf)). The facilitator run of show for the live quiz block is in [`docs/live-quiz.md`](docs/live-quiz.md).

## 1. Who can take the exams

Eligibility comes first, because it determines whether the rest of this document is relevant to you:

- **"Certification is available to people at Claude Partner Network organizations."** There is no publicly described path for individuals to register outside of a partner organization.
- Registration requires a partner email address on a recognized company domain. A personal email address (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) does not work for registration.

*(Source for both points: Anthropic Partner Academy, Certification FAQ, retrieved 14 September 2026: https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications)*

**What this means in practice:**

- **If you work at a company that is already a Claude Partner Network member:** ask your Academy admin to add you with your company email, then register for an exam through the Anthropic Partner Academy.
- **If your organization is not yet a CPN member:** it needs to join the Claude Partner Network first (membership is free and open to any organization that brings Claude to market), and you then register with its company email domain. Apply here: **https://claude.com/form/cpn-partner-application**. Existing partners manage their account through the Partner Hub: **https://partnerhub.anthropic.com/**. (Source: https://claude.com/partners, and https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network, published 12 March 2026, both retrieved 14 September 2026.)
- **Solo freelancers:** the FAQ above describes no separate path for them. If this applies to you, ask via the CPN application form rather than assuming either way.

## 3. Exam logistics

- **Delivery:** Pearson VUE, either OnVUE online proctored or an in-person test center. (Source: FAQ; https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html)
- **Format:** multiple choice and multiple response; each item states how many answers it requires. (Source: all four exam guides above)
- **Session length:** 120 minutes of exam time, roughly 135 minutes total seated time including instructions and the agreement. (Source: FAQ)
- **Language:** English only; using a browser translation tool during the exam is not allowed. (Source: FAQ)
- **Retakes:** up to 4 attempts within a rolling 12-month period. Waiting periods are 14 days after a 1st attempt, 30 days after a 2nd, and 90 days after a 3rd. Every retake is charged the full exam fee again. (Source: FAQ; Pearson VUE page above)
- **Validity and renewal:** a certification is valid for 12 months from the date it is earned. Renew before it lapses through a free, non-proctored renewal assessment; after it lapses, the full paid exam is required again. (Source: FAQ; exam guide, Policies and Logistics section)
- **Rescheduling:** reschedule or cancel at least 48 hours before your appointment; a no-show forfeits the fee. (Source: FAQ)
- **Discounts:** Registered-tier partners pay the full fee. Select, Preferred and Global Premier partners get 50% off, applied at checkout; through 31 December 2026 Global Premier partners get 100% off. (Source: FAQ)
- **Credential:** issued digitally through Credly by Pearson. (Source: https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications)
- **Practice exam:** the practice exam that was available on the previous platform was retired in the move to Pearson. There is no official standalone practice exam right now; the exam guides linked above include official sample questions instead. (Source: FAQ)
- **Excluded locations:** OnVUE online proctoring is not available in Belarus, Cuba, North Korea, Russia, Syria, and restricted regions of Ukraine; candidates with an ID from these places take the exam at a Pearson test center instead. Iran has its own, separate policy, described in the FAQ. (Source: FAQ)

## 4. The Claude Partner Network in short

- **How to join:** apply at https://claude.com/form/cpn-partner-application. Existing partners use the Partner Hub at https://partnerhub.anthropic.com/. Membership is free, and any organization that brings Claude to market is eligible to participate. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network, published 12 March 2026)
- **Entry tier:** "new applicants start at Registered, the program's entry level, with a minimum commitment to 10 certified practitioners." (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub, published 3 June 2026)
- **Tiers** (certified practitioners, joint customers in production trailing 12 months, public customer stories):

  | Tier | Certified practitioners | Joint customers in production | Public customer stories |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Registered | minimum commitment of 10 | not specified | not specified |
  | Select | 10 or more | 2 or more | 1 or more |
  | Preferred | 100 or more | 15 or more | 3 or more |
  | Global Premier | 1,000 or more | 100 or more, across 3 or more regions | 15 or more, plus a joint business plan with executive sponsors |

  (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub)

- **Tier review:** the next tier review is scheduled for 1 October 2026. (Source: https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications, published 23 July 2026)
- **What counts toward tier progress:** three metrics, updated daily in the Partner Hub: people actively certified who have used Claude in the last 90 days, joint customers in production over the trailing 12 months, and published customer stories. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub)
- **Which certifications count:** Developer Foundations, Architect Foundations, and Architect Professional count toward partner-tier certified-practitioner standing; Associate does not. (Source: FAQ and Certifications page above)
- **What membership brings:** direct financial support for training, sales enablement, and market development, with an initial commitment of 100 million USD for 2026; a partner portal with training and sales playbooks; listing in the Services Partner Directory for qualified partners; access to Applied AI Engineers for live deals, technical architects, local go-to-market support, and a Code Modernization Starter Kit; the exam discounts listed in section 3; and priority access to new certifications. (Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-partner-network; https://www.anthropic.com/news/services-track-partner-hub)

## 5. How to prepare

**Start with the official material, it is free and it is what the exam is actually written against.**

1. **Anthropic Partner Academy learning path** (Skilljar), described as "an introductory foundation to the Claude Partner Network and the first step toward unlocking certification": https://anthropic.skilljar.com/page/claude-partner-network-learning-path
   - Introduction to agent skills
   - Building with the Claude API
   - Introduction to Model Context Protocol
   - Claude Code in Action

   Further free courses are available in the general Anthropic Academy catalog: https://anthropic.skilljar.com/

2. **Exam guides and their official sample questions.** Each guide linked in section 2 lists the exam domains and their weighting, task statements, in-scope and out-of-scope topics, and a handful of official sample questions with explanations. Read the guide, especially the out-of-scope list, before you study anything else. We deliberately do not reproduce any sample questions here; open the PDF for the exam you are taking.
